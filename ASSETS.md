# ASSETS.md — Image & Audio Checklist

Everything the website needs from you, in the order I will ask for it.

**You do not need to gather all of this now.** The site is being built with elegant
placeholders in every slot, so it works start to finish with zero photographs. Drop files
in as you find them and each one appears in place.

---

## How to add a photograph

1. Find the row in the tables below. It gives you the **exact filename** to use.
2. Save your photo with that filename into the folder shown in the section heading.
3. That's it. No code, no renaming anything else.

**Technical notes, kept brief:**

- `.jpg` is expected. If you only have `.png` or `.heic`, send it to me and I'll convert it —
  HEIC (the iPhone default) will not display in a browser, so it must be converted.
- **Don't pre-crop or resize.** Send the largest original you have; I'll optimise every
  image for fast mobile loading as part of the build.
- Filenames are **lowercase, no spaces**. Keep them exactly as written.

## BATCH 3 — The photo galleries  ○ OPTIONAL BUT LOVELY

Chapter 04, *Little Pieces of Forever*, is the scrapbook. These are the only places where
you'll want a **quantity** of photographs. Each gallery works with as few as **two**
photos, and looks full at the counts below.

Filenames are sequential: `photo-01.jpg`, `photo-02.jpg`, `photo-03.jpg`, and so on. Add
as many as you like — galleries grow automatically. Captions for each one go in
`CONTENT.md`, not here.

| Folder | Page | Suggested count | What belongs here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 📁 `public/assets/memories/childhood/` | Once Upon a Childhood | 4–8 | Her own childhood and youth. Old scanned photos are perfect — the frames are designed for vintage prints, and imperfections look intentional. |
| 📁 `public/assets/memories/adventures/` | Our Little Adventures | 6–10 | Family trips, holidays, outings, journeys. Landscape works best. |
| 📁 `public/assets/memories/ordinary-days/` | Ordinary Days, Extraordinary Memories | 4–8 | Candids. Blurry, badly lit, phone photos — genuinely the right choice for this page. |
| 📁 `public/assets/memories/family/` | The Family Album | 6–12 | Group and family photographs. Shown in a tidy grid, so a consistent orientation looks best. |
| 📁 `public/assets/memories/stories/` | If These Pictures Could Talk | 3–5 | **Choose these carefully.** Only photos that have a real story behind them — each opens full-screen with your written story beside it. |

**Scanning old prints:** a phone photo of a print, taken flat in good daylight, is
completely fine. Don't worry about the edges; the frames cover them.
